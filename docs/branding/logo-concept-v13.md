# RecastPress logo concept v13

User clarification: use v12's current LEFT root curve as the symmetry reference, and make the neck less pinched.

The v12 left shoulder's 45-unit circular profile is retained and mirrored across the droplet axis x = 324.539823 for the right shoulder. Instead of merely matching curvature radii at different heights, the two visible shoulders now share mirrored positions. A separate smooth return curve connects the higher side back into the sloping circular frame.

Both shoulder arcs are shortened slightly at the neck, ending at ±20 degrees from their previous extreme points. This widens the neck from approximately 14 units to approximately 19.4 units and softens the transition into the approved rounded terminal. The terminal's main curves, position and height are retained.

Typeface, letter position, frame outer geometry, common circular cut, and glyph-confined square pixel boundary remain unchanged. Verification checks stream / letter separation, mask containment, and the shared cut circle.
