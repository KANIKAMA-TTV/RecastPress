# RecastPress logo concept v14

Redo of the root connection, using v12 as the base. The extra reverse bend introduced in v13 is removed. Each root side now uses a single smooth cubic transition from the circular band to a vertical neck. The neck is widened from approximately 14 to 22 units, and the right terminal-to-neck transition is one continuous cubic without an intermediate corner.

The two neck-side root controls have matching vertical tangents and matching control lengths. The frame's sloped edge requires different attachment heights; the root is balanced locally without inserting an extra hump to force a literal mirror.

The approved terminal's position and overall dimensions are retained. Font, glyph position, common circular cut and glyph-confined square pixels remain unchanged. Validation checks a clear gap between stream and letter, zero boundary pixels outside the glyph, and one shared cut circle.
