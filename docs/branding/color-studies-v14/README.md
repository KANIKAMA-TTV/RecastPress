# RecastPress colour and finish studies

10 flat background colours and 3 metal finishes. All based on concept v14.

Open background-colors.html and metallic-finishes.html for the visual sheets. Each mark links to its standalone SVG. Corresponding PNG files are included.

For M1 and M3, the letter, annular band and hanging stream receive the metal finish. M2 applies its brushed gunmetal finish to the circular ground, retaining the white mark. No glyph outline, cut mask, stream outline or ring proportion is changed. The SVG files preserve editable geometry and require no installed font.
