## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Make a major composition revision of this RecastPress logo, following the designer's specific corrections. Keep the deep blue circular badge, white classical Roman serif R and white tilted casting bucket, opaque white exterior background. REQUIRED: bucket and R must now be approximately the SAME VISUAL SIZE and have equal visual weight; the bucket is no longer a tiny accessory. Arrange a very large tilted bucket in the upper-left half and the R in the lower-right half, with a short thick flowing stream from the bucket's lowered right lip into the LEFT TOP of the R. The bucket including its handle should have a bounding box approximately 90-100 percent the height and width of the R bounding box. Both subjects must be bold and easily readable at 32px overall logo size. The bucket body is a simple solid white trapezoid silhouette with a single heavy arched handle and one bold blue opening indicating its lip, minimize fine outlines. The R retains a dignified inscriptional Roman serif style, thick vertical stem, curved bowl and diagonal leg. REQUIRED SECOND CHANGE: take a LARGE bite out of the UPPER LEFT of the R, roughly one quarter of the original letter area: its entire top-left serif and the upper portion of its left upright are still missing because the metal casting is unfinished. Show a large clean diagonal or one-step fill boundary across that upper-left corner, blue visible where the white letter is missing. The pouring stream joins this large unfinished edge. Restore the previous little notch in the upper-right bowl: the upper-right bowl must now be complete, the only incomplete area is clearly the upper LEFT. The letter must still read R from its remaining bowl, upright lower stem, serifs and diagonal leg. The big missing portion should remain visible even at thumbnail size; do not merely add small chips or distress. Keep ample blue separation around shapes and around the circle perimeter. Perfect full circle centered with equal generous white margins. Flat two-color vector-like logo, solid uniform opaque #24627A blue, solid white shapes, no gradients, no textures, no translucent patches, no 3D, no shadows. No words, captions, labels or comparison panels. One resolved logo mark.
