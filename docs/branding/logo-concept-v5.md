## Droplets and incomplete stem prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Edit the provided RecastPress logo v4. Preserve the muted publishing-blue circular badge, blue outer ring, white annular gap, white Roman serif R, opaque white square background. Apply ALL of these designer changes clearly: (1) Replace the three small white drops with EXACTLY TWO substantially LARGER white teardrops, about 1.8 times the prior droplet width and height. (2) INTERWEAVE the droplets with the circular border: align the two drops above the left upright of R. The top drop must physically overlap/intersect the upper-left arc of the white circular band, emerging from the blue outer ring and passing through the white annular gap into the blue inner disk. Integrate the shapes as a deliberate visual interlock: the white band narrows or opens around the droplet contour with slim blue negative-space separation along its sides so the teardrop silhouette remains visible; the white ring should read as feeding into or becoming a falling drop. The second, lower drop is fully inside the blue disk, directly below the first, separated by a clear blue gap, pointing toward the R's missing upright. Do not leave both drops floating entirely inside an intact circle; their relationship with the FRAME is a key requirement. Keep the overall circular outline readable. Both drops have pointed tops and rounded bottoms, no splashes or continuous pouring stream. The vertical upright should visibly begin around one third of the way down the letter height, with a broad clean slanted fill edge; the blue background must visibly occupy the upper third where that white stem used to be. The upper right curved bowl stays intact and readable, extending up to original cap height, and the diagonal leg and bottom serifs remain. The result still clearly reads a dignified classical Roman serif R, but visibly unfinished and being filled by the drops. The previous draft's small clipped corner is insufficient: remove a full third of the left upright. Keep R large and dominant; preserve existing letter scale. Crisp flat two-color artwork, uniform opaque blue #24627A and white only, no texture or gradients, no 3D, no shadows, no transparency. Generous equal white canvas margins around the circle.
