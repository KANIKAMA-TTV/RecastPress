# RecastPress logo concept v2

Direction: retain the Roman serif R and blue medallion. An unfinished section on the upper-right bowl and a tilted casting bucket pouring into that section communicate casting in progress.

Created with the built-in image_gen tool, editing concept v1. This is a raster design exploration. Flat solid colors and small-size simplification remain production refinements.

## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Edit the attached RecastPress concept. Preserve the classical Roman inscriptional serif R, deep muted publishing blue circular disk, white R, and balanced editorial/printer's-mark character. Two essential changes requested by designer: (1) the R is visibly unfinished because it is being cast right now: remove a modest but clearly visible irregular angular section of its upper-right curved bowl, about 10-15 percent of that stroke, revealing the blue behind. Keep the letter instantly recognizable as R and keep the diagonal leg and left vertical stem intact. The missing portion has a crisp stepped molten fill edge, as an incompletely filled type mold, not distressed antique texture or random damage. (2) Replace the abstract little bead and Y-shaped funnel with a real recognizable simplified casting bucket/crucible above the R: a small tilted trapezoid vessel with a thick lip and a single arched handle, pouring a short continuous white stream of liquid metal from its lowered lip into the unfinished upper bowl. Bucket and stream are flat white silhouettes inside the upper portion of the blue disk, sized at about one fifth of disk diameter; unmistakably a tipped bucket pouring downward, with blue negative space in its handle. Leave a small clear gap between bucket and R apart from the pouring stream, keep visual hierarchy R first bucket second. Redistribute upper whitespace as necessary without making the R too small. Remove the existing funnel and bead completely. The story should read 'casting an R from above'. Do not add a hand, person, tools, flames, sparks or splashes. Single standalone logo symbol, no text except the R, no wordmark, no labels, no comparison panel. Flat vector-like graphic, crisp clean edges, uniform solid #24627A blue fill and solid opaque white shapes, no gradients, no texture, no lighting or 3D. Genuinely transparent outside the circular disk, opaque blue throughout its interior. Square canvas with generous 10 percent transparent margin.
