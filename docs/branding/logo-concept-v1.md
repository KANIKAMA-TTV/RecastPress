# RecastPress logo concept v1

Status: design exploration; raster concept, not final vector artwork.

Direction: a classical Roman serif R in a deep blue circular medallion. A pouring funnel and short channel above the stem suggest casting a metal printing type. The inner border was removed to give the symbol a simpler, independent silhouette.

Generated using the built-in image_gen tool. The requested flat color still contains subtle tonal variation in the generated raster; production artwork should use solid fills. At small sizes, the bead and casting channel may need simplification.

## Initial prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one original logo concept for RecastPress, a publishing software brand. Primary request: a classical Roman serif capital R being cast from above in molten metal. A dignified, highly legible inscriptional Roman R with strongly defined wedge serifs and elegant diagonal leg; typographic, balanced, authoritative, like a traditional printer's mark. A circular medallion in deep muted publishing blue (#24627A), with an ivory-white R as negative space. At twelve o'clock a narrow opening in the medallion and a very short, beautifully controlled pouring stream enters from above and joins the upper left stem of the R, suggesting molten metal solidifying into a cast letter. The casting idea must be visibly integrated into the letter itself, not a separate illustration. One small detached teardrop above the stream is enough; restrained, geometric and refined, not splashy or messy. Keep the R counter clearly open and the silhouette recognizable at favicon size. The circular enclosure should be deliberately original, with one substantial single ring and distinctive proportions; do not reproduce or trace the WordPress W, WordPress logo, its exact ring construction, or its typography. Similar broad mood of traditional editorial software identity, but independent visual identity. Flat vector-like graphic, just deep blue and white, razor-sharp clean edges, no gradients, no texture, no 3D, no metallic rendering, no shadows, no mockup. Center the isolated mark large on a genuinely transparent square canvas with generous margin. No wordmark, no captions, no additional lettering, only the capital R. Produce a single resolved symbol, not a concept sheet.

## Refinement prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Refine the previous RecastPress logo into a clean and distinctive final concept. Preserve the beautifully proportioned, classical Roman serif capital R and deep publishing-blue circular medallion. Remove the entire white inner circular border/ring: the only white shape inside the blue disk should be the R and its integrated casting channel. Make all blue areas a completely uniform solid #24627A, completely opaque within the disk; remove all mottling, discoloration, textures, gradients and accidental transparency holes visible in the previous image. At the very top edge, slightly left of center and aligned exactly with the left upright stem of R, place a small simple V-shaped pouring funnel in white, connecting down through a short narrow vertical white casting channel into the top serif of the R. This represents molten type metal poured into a letter mold from above, NOT a flame, fountain, candle, splash or water logo. A tiny detached single white bead of metal just above that funnel; keep plenty of transparent margin above the bead and around the disk. The R must read immediately and dominate, with a traditional inscriptional serif silhouette and a clear enclosed counter. Use the circle without any concentric rings, differentiate from WordPress. Perfectly crisp flat vector-like design, no visual effects, no mockup, no background, genuinely transparent outside the mark only. Uniform opaque blue disk, solid white R. One logo symbol only, no title, no wordmark, no other text. Square canvas, logo occupies 75 percent of canvas width.
